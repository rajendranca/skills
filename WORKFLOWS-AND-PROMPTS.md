# Everyday Copilot workflows — copy into VS Code Chat
Use the model picker for a strong frontier model for architecture and difficult cross-pod reasoning. Use a lighter/Auto mode for drafting. The toolkit deliberately does not pin any specific model.

## 1. When a new intake arrives (intake-triage-lead)
> Here is a new request: [paste approved intake]. Decide if this needs operational escalation, quick triage, focused discovery, or formal project intake. Explain why. Identify impacted domains, proposed coordinating owner, minimal stakeholders, missing evidence and the next decision gate. Do not assign resources or dates.

## 2. Chief architect's challenge (portfolio-chief-architect)
> Read the architecture notes/diagrams in this workspace. Identify 5 assumptions that could hurt reliability, integration, security, operability or cross-application ownership. Compare two credible alternatives and produce a decision-ready recommendation with evidence references. No edits.

## 3. Pod execution review (pod-delivery-governor)
> Review this week's pod notes and integrated delivery report. Identify cross-pod dependencies, critical-path uncertainty, architecture drift, QA/release blockers and the three escalations that require my decision. Suggest what my delivery manager, senior managers, chief QA lead and pod leads should own. Distinguish commitment from planning estimate.

## 4. Stakeholder meeting preparation (stakeholder-and-delegation-advisor)
> I need to align stakeholders for [change]. Who should be in the first 30-minute working session vs consulted offline? Draft objective, 4-point agenda, decisions and suggested owner actions. Label RACI as proposed.

## 5. Architectural decision (architecture-governance-chair)
> Evaluate [option A] vs [option B] for [context]. Consider integration, HA/DR, operations, performance, security, migration cost, team capability and delivery confidence. Use available evidence only, draft an ADR and identify mandatory review gates.

## 6. QA oversight without micromanagement (qa-strategist)
> Given this release/design note, challenge completeness of the QA strategy for integration, regression, resilience, soak/performance, monitoring and rollback. Give questions for my chief QA lead and a risk-based evidence checklist, not speculative signoff.

## 7. Leadership update (executive-portfolio-briefing)
> Convert these approved architecture and pod status notes into a 90-second executive brief: progress, material change, decisions required, top two risks and what support I need. Avoid invented commitments or status colors.

## 8. Agentic development adoption (agentic-transformation-lead)
> Design a small controlled agentic SDLC pilot for one pod with spec-driven development, an implementation agent, independent QA, CI evidence and human PR approvals. Define autonomy levels, policy controls, evaluation tasks, baselines and go/no-go scale criteria.

## 9. Meeting outcome (portfolio-chief-architect)
> Turn these minutes into confirmed decisions, proposed decisions, risks, dependencies and action owners by role. Identify every decision or action that lacks authority/owner. Draft a concise follow-up without sending anything.

## 10. 1:1 preparation (people-leader)
> Help me plan a constructive 1:1 with a senior manager about [behavior/outcome]. Stick to observed examples, collaborative questions, growth expectations and follow-up. Do not infer personal motives or access private HR data.
