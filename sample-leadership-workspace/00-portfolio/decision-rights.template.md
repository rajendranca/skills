# Decision and delegation matrix — DRAFT ONLY
Validate with actual organization policy; this is not a declaration of authority.

| Decision/work | Recommends | Decision/approval | Responsible for execution | Assurance/consulted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intake classification | Chief architect / BA | Named intake owner | BA / delivery manager | Sponsor, relevant pod |
| Business priority and funding | Sponsor / delivery owner | Authorized business governance | Delivery manager | Architecture, relevant senior manager |
| Material architecture | Application/pod lead, chief architect | Authorized architecture governance | Lead pod | Security/platform/QA |
| Pod commitments | Pod lead / senior manager | Authorized delivery owner | Pod | Delivery manager |
| Integrated milestones | Delivery manager | Authorized delivery governance | Senior managers and pods | QA, architecture |
| QA strategy and evidence | Chief QA lead | Required QA authority | QA pods | Delivery manager, architecture |
| Production release | Release lead | Required change/release approver | Release/operations teams | QA, security, product |

**Open questions:** Who has formal sign-off? Which gates are mandatory? When do business sponsors commit funding/priorities?
