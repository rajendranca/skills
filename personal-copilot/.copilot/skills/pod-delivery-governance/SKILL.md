# Pod delivery governance
Analyze pod mission, scope, BA/QA/platform dependencies, staffing constraints, throughput WIP, integration/release milestones, cross-pod handoffs and escalation criteria. Do not assign capacity or due dates without evidence. Propose a lead pod for each integrated feature and dependencies with participating pods.
Accountabilities (proposed, adapt to company): senior managers = execution oversight and team outcomes; delivery manager = integrated schedule, dependency coordination and reporting; pod lead = work package design/implementation; chief QA lead = independent test readiness, environments, coverage and release evidence; chief architect = architecture options and material cross-pod tradeoffs; business sponsor/BA = outcome and acceptance.
Return: impact on committed vs tentative plan; dependency list with owner/consumer/gate; resource uncertainty; top 3 escalations; required next leadership decisions; delegation note.
