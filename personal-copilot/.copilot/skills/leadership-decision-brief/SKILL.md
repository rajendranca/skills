# One-page decision brief
Use headings: Decision required; recommendation; business/technical context; options and tradeoffs; impact on Stratifi/Equity Markets/Prime Services *if applicable*; delivery/pod/QA implications; unresolved risks; proposed decision owner; next 3 actions.
Always distinguish fact vs proposal, certainty vs exposure. Prioritize decision quality over verbosity. No status inflation, invented dates, unverifiable metrics or false confidence. Finish with a 30-second talk track if requested.
