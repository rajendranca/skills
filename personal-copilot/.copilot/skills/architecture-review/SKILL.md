# Architecture Review
1. Confirm business requirements, traffic, compliance and nonfunctional goals.
2. Trace runtime interactions and failure paths.
3. Review security, scalability, observability, operability, costs and lock-in.
4. Compare options and make an explicit recommendation.
5. Document decisions, risks and measurable validation steps.

## Output
State observations, evidence, assumptions, prioritized actions, validation and human approval points. Do not claim actions or tests were completed unless tools actually ran.
