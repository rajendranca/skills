# Production Readiness
Check security, capacity, service ownership, dashboards, alerts, on-call, dependencies, backout, database migration, credentials, DR, support handoff, runbooks and evidence. Flag unknowns as unknown, not passes. Produce go/no-go with conditions and named owners to assign.

## Output
State observations, evidence, assumptions, prioritized actions, validation and human approval points. Do not claim actions or tests were completed unless tools actually ran.
