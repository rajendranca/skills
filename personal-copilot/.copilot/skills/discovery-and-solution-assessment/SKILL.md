# Discovery assessment
Structure a short discovery brief: problem/current pain, measurable business outcome, users/consumers, in/out scope, business rules, data sources, integration contracts, affected apps, NFRs, controls, migration and reversibility, risks, non-goals, options, rough order-of-magnitude effort only with grounded assumptions. Separate facts, user claims, hypotheses, and validations needed.
Ask what the sponsor must decide (proceed, reject, park, split, spike, fund), what the architecture owner needs to validate, what the QA lead must test, and what delivery manager/senior managers must assess on timing and pod capacity.
Outputs: executive recommendation, discovery agenda, questions by stakeholder, options/decision criteria, concrete exit criteria before sizing/commitment, and **proposed** handoff into a pod or program.
Never manufacture estimates, costs, calendar dates or approvals.
