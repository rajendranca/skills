# Decision rights and delegation
1. State decision/issue and why it requires attention.
2. Separate **recommend**, **decide/approve**, **execute**, **assure** and **inform**. Treat company RACI as the source of truth, and label suggested RACI as provisional.
3. Default proposal (not policy): portfolio chief architect challenges/endorses material architecture; business sponsor prioritizes business value; delivery manager coordinates integrated plan; senior manager supervises pod execution and staffing; pod lead owns implementation; chief QA lead assures test strategy/evidence; release/control approvers decide within their own governance.
4. Escalation triggers: incompatible architecture choices, high-severity operational/control risk, an unowned dependency, QA/release gate failure, capacity conflict requiring reprioritization, or external business commitment risk.
5. Return a delegation brief with issue, proposed decision owner, executor, evidence package, escalation trigger and follow-up checkpoint (only if supplied).
Do not equate the user's accountability with personally doing each task.
