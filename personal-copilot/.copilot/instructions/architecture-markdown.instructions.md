---
name: 'Architecture and leadership Markdown'
description: 'Use when drafting or modifying architecture, intake, delivery, QA, or leadership Markdown documents.'
applyTo: '**/*.md'
---
- Put decision, evidence and action at the top; details below.
- Use proposed vs approved status accurately and distinguish open assumptions.
- Favor concise headings, Markdown tables when useful, and named role owners rather than fake assignments.
- Never invent enterprise platform interfaces, pod commitments, regulator requirements or performance data.
- For diagrams, label inferred/proposed flows separately from confirmed flows.
