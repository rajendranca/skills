# Future team-wide rollout — OPTIONAL (not installed with personal kit)
Do not distribute personal leadership/HR agents to repositories automatically.

Stage 1: personal read-only decision support; obtain corporate permission for model/data classification.
Stage 2: review/distribute only generic skills for intake/architecture/QA in a shared `.github/skills` or organization customization source; remove proprietary role assumptions.
Stage 3: one pod pilots agentic SDLC: spec/plan → supervised implementation on isolated branch → CI/QA review → authorized human merge/change control.
Stage 4: adopt scorecards for correctness, review effort, cycle time, cost, auditability and defects. Validate with chief QA lead, delivery manager, security, senior managers and engineering governance before scaling.

Avoid coupling HR or people coaching content with code-generation agents. Formal approval and operational control are not delegated to agents.
